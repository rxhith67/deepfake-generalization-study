Assessment Task 3: Project Research Presentation
Due 23 Oct by 23:59 Points 25 Submitting a file upload
This task will involve each student presenting a critical review of an academic research paper related to the topic studied in Assessment Tasks 1 and 2. The student is expected to critically appraise the work in terms of the techniques applied, evaluation methods and experimental results based on knowledge obtained in the subject.
